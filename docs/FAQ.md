# Promise Tracker FAQ

## Getting started

### How do I sign in?

Select Google sign-in. Promise Tracker currently supports Google accounts only. After you choose an account, you are returned to the app.

### Why did I see Google account selection after logging out?

Logging out clears the Promise Tracker browser session. Because Google is the only sign-in method, the app then asks you to choose a Google account again.

### How do I change the name people see?

Click your profile at the bottom of the sidebar, enter a display name, and save. That name appears on promises you create in group spaces.

## Promises

### What is the difference between a recurring promise and a date-range promise?

A recurring promise starts a new progress period each day, week, or month. A date-range promise uses one cumulative total between its selected start and end dates.

### Why is a completed daily promise available again later?

Daily, weekly, and monthly promises reset automatically for their next period. Your older check-ins remain in Progress history.

### How do I log a note with progress?

Open a promise card and select **Mark done** or **Log progress**. The check-in form includes an optional Note field.

### Where can I see a graph and earlier check-ins?

Open a promise’s **Manage Promise** panel and choose **View progress & graph**. The history shows dated entries, notes, and a chart. Recurring promises emphasize the current period while retaining earlier entries below it.

### Can I edit a promise?

Yes. Open **Manage Promise** and choose **Edit promise details**. Promise owners can change the title, category, recurrence, target/unit where applicable, and motivation text.

### What happens when I archive a promise?

It moves to the **Archived** tab and keeps all history. It is not deleted.

### How do I restore an archived promise?

Open the **Archived** tab, open **Manage Promise**, then choose **Restore to active promises**.

## Groups

### How do I invite someone?

Open the group and select **Invite people**. Copy the generated link and send it to the person. They must sign in with Google before joining.

### What happens with “Admin approval required”?

The invitee submits a join request. Group owners and admins see it in **Group settings** and can approve or decline it.

### Who can see group promises?

All current members of that private group can see its promises, owners, progress, notes, and history. Keep private commitments in your personal space instead.

### How do I see who is an owner, admin, or member?

Open **Group settings**. The Members list shows every member and their role.

### Who can change roles?

Only the group owner can promote a member to admin or return an admin to member.

### Who can delete a group?

Only the owner can delete a group. Deletion is permanent and removes the group’s promises, progress, invitations, join requests, and membership records.

### Can I filter a group’s promises?

Yes. Use **All categories** to filter by category and **All members** to filter by the display name of the promise owner.

## Troubleshooting

### I see “Could not load groups” or “Could not load promises.” What should I do?

Refresh once after a deployment finishes. If it persists, sign out through Profile and sign back in. If the issue continues, report the message and the approximate time so the service logs can be checked.

### My invite link does not add me immediately.

If the group requires admin approval, your request is pending until an owner or admin approves it. Otherwise, confirm that you opened the full copied link and completed Google sign-in.

### Can I receive reminders or Google Calendar check-ins yet?

Not yet. Browser/mobile reminders and Google Calendar group check-ins are planned next-stage features.
